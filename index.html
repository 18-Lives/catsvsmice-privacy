Cats vs Mice

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

In short: Cats vs Mice does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. Your progress stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The one optional purchase is handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Who we are

Cats vs Mice is made by 18 Lives LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not create an account for you. It does not connect to the internet to play, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, so we have nothing about you to collect, share or sell.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a save file on your device. It holds game data only, such as:

  • rooms cleared, stars, best times and achievements;
  • your daily level results and streak;
  • your settings, such as volume, language, text size and whether reminders are on;
  • whether you own the Supporter Pack, and which cosmetics you have chosen.

This file never leaves your device through the game and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress, and it is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your device (for example with iCloud), the save file may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Reminders

If you choose to turn them on, the game can remind you about your daily streak. These are local notifications: your device schedules and shows them itself, and no notification service, server or third party is involved. The game only asks for permission after you say you want reminders. You can turn them off in the game under Settings → Streak reminders, or in your device's settings for the app.

Purchases

Cats vs Mice is free to play. The game offers one optional, one-time purchase, the Supporter Pack, which unlocks cosmetic items such as hats for your cats. It does not change how the game plays.

If you buy the Supporter Pack, the purchase is handled entirely by the store you downloaded the game from: Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your name, payment details, billing address or purchase history. The only thing the game learns from the store is whether the Supporter Pack is owned, yes or no. That answer is kept in the save file on your device so your cosmetics still work when you are offline. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

How Apple and Google handle your payment information is covered by their own policies: Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Cats vs Mice is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding a feature that needs information it does not use today, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
