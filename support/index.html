How does the daily streak work?

Daily Level on the main menu has a new room every day, open from the moment you install the game. Win it on the day and your streak grows by one. Miss a day and the streak starts again from zero. A day runs from midnight to midnight on your phone's clock.

Losing does not break the streak. There is no limit on attempts, so you can keep trying until midnight. A run you start before midnight but win after it counts as a replay of the day before, so it does not add to the streak.

Can I play days I missed?

Yes. Open Awards, Stats & Streaks on the main menu and go to the Streak tab. Tap any past day on the calendar to play or replay it. Those wins are recorded on the calendar, but only a win on the day itself adds to the streak.

Why am I not getting streak reminders?

  • Check the game: in Settings & More, Streak reminders should be On. If you said no when your phone first asked, switching it on takes you to your phone's settings so you can allow notifications there.
  • Check your phone. On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Cats vs Mice. On Android: Settings → Apps → Cats vs Mice → Notifications.
  • Streak reminders come at about 7 pm on days your streak is still waiting for today's win, or a little later if you were playing at 7. Once today is won, there is nothing to remind you about until tomorrow.

Why did I get a notification when I don't have a streak?

If reminders are on and you haven't opened the game for three days, it sends one note to say the mice are back. Switching Streak reminders off in Settings & More stops both kinds of reminder.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Your progress is saved only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it.

  • iPhone: if your iPhone is backed up (for example with iCloud), restoring that backup should bring your progress back.
  • Android: the game's progress is not included in Android backups, so once the app is deleted it cannot be recovered.
  • The Supporter Pack is never lost. It belongs to your Apple or Google account, and the game checks for it each time it starts. If it doesn't show up, open Cosmetics and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I start over?

Open Settings & More and choose Reset Progress at the bottom. The game asks three times before it deletes anything. A reset erases your stars, achievements, stats, runs in progress, streak and calendar history, Field Guide discoveries and earned hats, and it cannot be undone. Your settings and the Supporter Pack are kept.

What are Cosmetics and the Supporter Pack?

Cosmetics on the main menu is where you dress up your cats: hats, defeat effects and map tints. They change how the game looks, never how it plays.

Some are free: you earn a new hat for clearing rooms 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 of the campaign. The rest come with the Supporter Pack, a one-time purchase that helps fund the next game. Any cosmetics added later are free for pack owners.

  • New phone, or reinstalled the game? Open Cosmetics and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple or Google account you bought it with.
  • "Your purchase is pending": the pack unlocks as soon as the payment clears.
  • "The store can't be reached right now": check your connection and try again.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. A refunded pack is removed from the game.

When does Chaos mode unlock?

After you clear room 5 of the campaign. Chaos is endless: every third wave you choose a modifier, and every one you take stacks for the rest of the run.

How do I learn what beats each mouse?

Basics on the main menu covers how to play. The Field Guide has every mouse, mutation, cat and spell, with tips on how to counter each mouse.

Can I change the language or make the game easier to see?

Yes. Settings & More has a Language choice (English or Español; until you pick one, the game follows your phone's language), plus Large text, Colourblind mode and a Left-handed layout. You can also adjust sound, music, vibration and how many visual effects are shown.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
